Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Keep a finger on the screen and move it: your fencer follows. Sideways steps, up and down sets the blade's height. Flick quickly toward the opponent to thrust, or backward to leap away. Reach where their blade is not for a point; blades at a similar height clash. You can replay the tutorial any time from "How to play" on the title screen.

How do I get more swordsmen?

Each opponent you beat for the first time joins you as a swordsman. Pick one in "Swordsmen" to fight alone with its strength and weakness. One change a day is free; after that, watch an ad to change. Going back to Normal is always free.

Where is my data stored?

Your records, rank, swordsmen and settings are all stored only on your device. No account is needed and nothing is sent to a server.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Please note that deleting the app also deletes your data.

How do I change the language or the sound?

Open "Settings" on the title screen. You can choose the language, turn sound effects and music on or off, adjust the controls and turn screen effects on or off.

Watching an ad doesn't let me continue

Continuing, retrying and changing swordsman work only after an ad has been watched to the end. Without a connection (in airplane mode, for example) no ad can load, so they can't be used. Please try again with a connection.

About ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: video ads you choose to watch, and a full-screen ad now and then after a duel ends. No ads appear during a duel. See the privacy policy for details.

Are there in-app purchases?

No. The whole game is free to play.
