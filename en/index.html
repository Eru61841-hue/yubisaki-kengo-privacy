Privacy Policy

App: Kengo Duel (指先剣豪) / Effective: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Kengo Duel ("the app") respects your privacy and handles information as described below.

2. Data the app handles

Game data such as your best records, your rank, the swordsmen you have collected, Daily Duel results and your settings is stored only on your device. No account is needed, and this data is never sent to the developer or to third parties.

The app itself does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: video ads that play only when you choose to watch one (to continue in 100 Duels, to retry the Daily Duel, or to change swordsman a second time in a day), and a full-screen ad shown now and then after a duel ends. No ads are shown during a duel.

To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier and usage data, under their own privacy policies.

On iOS, the app may ask for permission to track, for example when it is first opened. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not tailored to you. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings app.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing your result

When you tap "Share" on a result screen, a short text with your record and a picture of the result are handed to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you tap it. The service you share to handles it under its own policies.

5. Device features

The app plays music and sound effects and uses a light vibration when you press buttons. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location or notifications.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device also deletes all game data stored on it.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when necessary. Any change will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact address on the support page.
